12 sample tips for comparing categories. These examples do not reflect the live set. Urgent tips use a dark category-colored background and light text, without an accent border.

Confirmed live-screen baseline

This mockup's default layout is the development baseline. The controls above the canvas let us review alternatives; they are not part of the production app.

Scoring and match context

  • Our scoring pad is on the left, theirs on the right. Full words are the default; paper codes remain an option.
  • The live score and completed-set scores sit above the court, without a title or match-context line. There is no separate header by default. Our team name opens the menu.
  • Our contacts take two taps, code-first or player-first; our ace and serve error use the known server in one tap. Their contacts take one tap. Team finishes an unattributed error.

Court and match actions

  • Jersey numbers show serving or receive formations from Where do I go? Court-view controls sit inside the court.
  • Our Sub, Libero, and Timeout controls sit below our pad; their Timeout sits below theirs.
  • Recent rallies and Undo stay at the bottom. Coach's corner shows up to three suggestions under the court, with additional suggestions one level deeper.
  • Planned swaps require confirmation. Production actions must all use the same eligibility checks and record explicit overrides.

Production recovery policy

  • Save each completed match action immediately and restore the match after reopening. Incomplete entries are not rallies.
  • Undo covers all match actions. Historical edits recalculate later state and flag conflicting substitutions or libero changes for review.
  • Manual corrections preserve the original record. This demo still keeps its state only in memory.

Coaching evidence and device checks

  • Production suggestions must distinguish terminal attacks from all swings, and back-row receiving rallies from actual passes.
  • Libero comparisons show observed rates and sample sizes, with cautious recommendations rather than precise errors-saved predictions. The demo's existing suggestion logic has not yet been updated to these decisions.
  • Test touch targets, reopening, offline tracking, wake lock, Google sign-in, and a sample Drive backup on the coach's iPad after the first persistent live-set build.
  • Review shortcuts: #pending, #base, #sub, #swap, #run, #crunch, #form, #libero, and #menu.
